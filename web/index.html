The science, honestly

Every gram traceable to a paper.

Your targets aren't guessed. The engine derives them from published nutrition research and keeps the citation attached, so a curious user can follow any number back to its source. We'd rather show our working than oversell precision.

Built for general healthy adults. Not a substitute for clinical nutrition guidance — if you have a diagnosed condition affecting your diet, talk to your doctor or dietitian.

Protein, quality-adjusted

A vegetarian hitting a protein number on dal is not the same as on paneer or egg. We score protein by quality, not just grams — so a plant-forward plate actually delivers.
