Disclosed clinical conditions (optional)

What checking a box actually does: it locks the matching constraint out of the planner entirely, so it is never loosened to find you a plan — even if loosening it is the only way to produce one. Hypertension locks your sodium ceiling; chronic kidney disease locks sodium and protein; diabetes locks carbohydrate. If a locked constraint is the reason no plan can be built, the planner will say so by name, plainly, on the final step — never a generic failure. This is a planning safeguard, not medical advice — see the science page for what "locked" does and doesn't mean.
